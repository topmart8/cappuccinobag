# Pet Travel Content Map

## Positioning

Pet Travel Bags is one additional functional-bag category under the existing positioning: custom outdoor, sports, travel and functional bag manufacturer in China. It does not replace or weaken racquet sports, outdoor, hiking, travel, wallet, factory proof or RFQ content.

## Route inventory

- 1 collection hub: `/pet-travel-bags/`

- 12 product pages: PT001–PT012

## Buyer journey

1. Homepage or navigation introduces the additional functional category.
2. Collection hub answers capabilities and presents categories/products.
3. Subcategories resolve format-specific commercial intent.
4. Product pages provide technical content, customization, sampling, QC and FAQ.
5. Buyer guides support research and link to relevant product pages.
6. Every path leads to RFQ, email, WhatsApp and factory proof.

## Content controls

- No fixed MOQ or lead-time promise.
- No airline-approved, TSA-approved, crash-tested or safety-certified claim.
- Airline-compatible wording includes a rule-confirmation caveat.
- Recycled and GRS wording is limited to documented material options.
- No medical, veterinary, rating, review, price, stock, GTIN or availability claim.
