# Cappuccino Product Release Plan — Batch 01

Status: evidence review before publication  
Cadence: 5 products per batch, twice weekly  
Target: 60 high-quality product pages across 12 batches

## Brand and evidence gates

Every product must:

- use Cappuccino's cream, forest green, coffee brown and charcoal visual system;
- remain visually and editorially distinct from Novlane;
- use a real sample, factory, material, construction or use-scene reference;
- avoid AI-looking hero images, invented specifications and generic scaled copy;
- remove platform watermarks, domestic marketplace references and supplier identities;
- state which specifications are confirmed and which remain sample-stage options;
- link search intent to buyer proof and one qualified inquiry action;
- pass mobile, metadata, schema, internal-link and image-alt checks before publication.

## Publication checklist

1. Confirm the actual product sample and image rights.
2. Record dimensions, materials, components and intended use.
3. Photograph the product in a credible use scene and include construction details.
4. Write the buyer problem, verifiable selling points, options and QC checkpoints.
5. Add one primary keyword, supporting buyer questions and natural internal links.
6. Connect the CTA to the existing inquiry form with a product-specific topic.
7. Review for unsupported performance, compliance, capacity or delivery claims.
8. Validate at 360, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels, then release only after evidence approval.

## Success measures

- Qualified organic visits by product and search intent
- Engagement with evidence, specification and FAQ sections
- Inquiry-form starts and completed qualified inquiries
- Buyer questions that reveal clearer product-market demand
- Search and AI-answer visibility for product-specific manufacturing questions
