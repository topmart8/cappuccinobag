FAQ

Can you support recycled fabrics?

Yes. We can review recycled polyester or nylon for certificate needs, color consistency, coating, and target cost.

Do you test with weight?

Load testing is recommended for outdoor packs, especially larger packs or products with hip belts.

Can you make urban-outdoor crossover packs?

Yes. Many buyers want trail-inspired packs that also work for commuting and travel.

Ready to brief a custom project?

Send references, order quantity, material direction, logo method, packaging needs, and sample deadline. We will suggest a practical route for quotation, sampling, and production.
