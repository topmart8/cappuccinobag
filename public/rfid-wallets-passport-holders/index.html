FAQ

Can RFID wallets be gift packed?

Yes. Gift boxes, insert cards, sleeves, barcode labels, and private-label packaging can be supported.

Can wallets match a bag collection?

Yes. Materials, trims, labels, and packaging can be coordinated with bags.

Which logo methods work best?

Debossing, foil stamping, woven labels, and small badges are common depending on material.
