About Cappuccino Bag

Cappuccino Bag is an OEM/ODM bag manufacturer specializing in outdoor sports bags, travel backpacks, racket bags, wallets, cardholders, and smart eco-friendly bags for global brands.

OEM/ODM bag development partner

Cappuccino Bag develops OEM/ODM bag programs for global brands across five official categories: Outdoor Sports Bags, Travel Backpacks, Racket Bags, Wallets & Cardholders, and Smart Eco-Friendly Bags.

The Wallets & Cardholders category includes RFID wallets, passport holders, cardholders, gift sets, and travel accessories designed for private-label programs.

Our work focuses on practical OEM/ODM steps: reviewing product references, choosing suitable materials, preparing samples, improving functional details, confirming logo methods, checking production quality, and arranging export-ready packing.
