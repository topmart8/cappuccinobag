What to Confirm Before Sampling

  • Equipment fit. Send paddle dimensions, paddle count, bottle dimensions and shoe size if the bag needs a footwear pocket.
  • Construction. Agree fabric, padding and pocket openings. Check zipper clearance and attachment reinforcement on the sample.
  • Branding and packaging. Send logo artwork, target placement, quantity, destination and any retail packing requirements.

Pickleball Bag Buyer FAQ

What is the minimum order for custom pickleball bags?

New custom production starts at 300 pieces per style. Different styles cannot be combined to meet the minimum. Sampling is assessed separately.

Can you make pickleball bags for clubs?

Yes. Share your club logo, target quantity, preferred bag format and packaging needs so we can review your project.

Can we develop a matching paddle sleeve?

Yes. A paddle sleeve can use coordinated colours, fabrics and branding. Send the paddle dimensions, including any edge guard or grip build-up, for fit review.

Can I add shoe storage, bottle retention or a fence hook?

Yes. Share the packed load and how the bag will be carried or hung. We review pocket placement and check clearances, retention and attachment reinforcement on the sample.
