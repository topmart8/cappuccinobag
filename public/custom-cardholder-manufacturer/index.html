FAQ

Can cardholders include RFID protection?

Yes. RFID cardholder projects can include a shielding layer, but the exact placement should be confirmed during sample review.

Can you make leather and vegan leather cardholders?

Yes. Material direction can be reviewed based on target price, handfeel, logo method, edge finish, and brand positioning.

What details matter most for cardholder QC?

Card slot tension, stitch alignment, edge finish, logo clarity, thickness, color consistency, and packing cleanliness should be checked.
