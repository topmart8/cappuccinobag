Sampling and revision workflow

A first sample should be tested with real contents and realistic handling. Rackets, paddles, shoes, bottles, passports, laptops, towels, apparel, or amenity products should be placed into the item exactly as customers will use it. Specific sample comments shorten the route to bulk approval.

How to write a better RFQ

A strong RFQ includes product type, dimensions, reference photos, required compartments, material preference, logo method, order quantity, target price level, packaging requirements, destination market, sample deadline, and compliance needs. If these details are not ready, start with the business goal and ask the factory to suggest a sample-ready route.
