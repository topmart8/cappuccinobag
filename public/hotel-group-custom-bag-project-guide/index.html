FAQ

Can you make multiple hotel bag items as one set?

Yes. Amenity totes, laundry bags, pouches, and organizers can share materials and trims.

Can branding be subtle?

Yes. Many hospitality projects use tonal embroidery, woven labels, or small embossed patches.

Can packing be sorted by property?

Yes. Packing and carton marks can be planned for property distribution.
