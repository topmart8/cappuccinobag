Cappuccino Bag Company Bio

Cappuccino Bag is the B2B OEM/ODM bag development and manufacturing brand of Guangzhou Cappuccino Leather Handbag Co., Ltd., focused on padel and racket-sports bags, outdoor and travel bags, wallets and cardholders.

Company bio

Cappuccino Bag develops OEM/ODM bag programs for global brands with a padel-first focus across: Padel & Racket Sports Bags, Outdoor & Travel Bags, Wallets & Cardholders, and project-specific material-innovation products.

Accessory category focus

The Wallets & Cardholders category includes RFID wallets, passport holders, cardholders, gift sets, and travel accessories designed for private-label programs.
