Buyer FAQ

Questions to settle before sampling

Can a padel bag match our racket colorway?

Yes. We can develop material, trim and logo directions from approved brand references, subject to sample confirmation.

Can you develop from a reference image or tech pack?

Yes. A tech pack gives the most reliable basis; an annotated reference image can establish direction.

What is needed before sampling?

Format, capacity, compartments, quantity, market, material, color, logo, packaging, sample date and reference files.

Can we customize shoe and thermal compartments?

Yes. Both can be reviewed during specification and sample development.

Can recycled polyester be considered?

Yes, subject to availability, structure, color and coating confirmation.

How does sample feedback reach bulk production?

Approved revisions are recorded in confirmed specifications, references and production checkpoints.
