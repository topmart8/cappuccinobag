Pickleball Backpack OEM Development Guide

Treat the first brief as an equipment envelope. Measure each paddle with its grip and cover, then list simultaneous loads and unavoidable access paths. Draw front, side, back and inside views and label each opening.

Move from brief to first article

Use a revision-numbered BOM and record seam and reinforcement locations. Have the sample maker mark the actual pocket opening and finished usable dimensions before the first load trial. Photograph the packed bag from both sides.

Freeze the approved revision

List each requested change with its location and acceptance condition. Review the next sample with the same load and save the approved materials, artwork, colour references and actual fit evidence together.
