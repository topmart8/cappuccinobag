How to Quote a Custom Padel Bag at 300 and 500 Pieces

Cappuccino Bag · 22 November 2026

Cappuccino Bag’s standard minimum for new custom padel bag production is 300 pieces per style. To compare 300 and 500 pieces, ask for the same bag specification, colour plan and trade term at both quantities. Have the factory list prototype development, logo setup, custom tooling and freight separately.

Can I test the design before committing to the bulk order?

Ask the factory which physical sample you can review and whether its construction matches the bag you plan to develop. For a quick workmanship check, request views of the seams, zipper ends, shoulder strap attachments and inside dividers. For a check of your specific layout, commission a development sample against a written brief. An existing sample can demonstrate the workmanship you see on that bag. Your final shoe and racket fit still depends on the layout and equipment you specify.

What do I send if I only have sketches?

Start with front and side references, target width, height and depth, and a list of what goes into every compartment. Include the target order quantity, colour split, logo artwork, destination and deadline. If you want a front racket pocket, show whether the whole racket including its handle must be enclosed. If a custom zipper puller or moulded part is needed, agree who provides the drawings, which file format will be used and when tooling can start.

Which charges belong on separate lines?

Quote lineWhat to ask the factory to confirm
Development and physical sampleWhat drawings, patterns, materials and sample rounds are included?
BrandingAre embroidery digitising and the stitched logo priced separately?
ToolingIs a new puller mould needed? Who can use it for repeat orders?
RevisionsWhat triggers an extra charge after the first sample?
Bulk and packagingWhat is the unit price at 300 and 500 with the same box, labels and packing specification?
ShippingWhich service and destination are quoted for the sample and for bulk?

If the factory offers a sample-fee credit, ask for the qualifying order quantity, eligible charges and timing in the pro forma invoice. Compare the total cost of the first order as well as the unit price.

Buyer question

How can I check a factory’s workmanship before paying for a prototype?

Ask for a close-up video of a physical bag showing seam joins, zipper ends, lining, load-bearing attachments and pocket access. Check which details match the construction proposed for your design.
