Modular Paddle Storage: Design Considerations

Draw the paddle module and host bag independently. Mark maximum paddle width and handle projection, the path needed to pass through the zip opening and the usable clearance around anchor points.

Choose where forces go

Decide whether the removable part carries load through its base or through the attachment tabs. Prototype the retention using common sewn webbing. Place hardware away from the paddle edge and check for rubbing against the lining.

Compare installed and removed states

Record the remaining usable space, access time and displaced equipment in both states. Test a full module in upright and inverted orientations. Keep wide clearance around any tab the user must release by hand.

Write an interface check a second sample maker can repeat

Mark two reference points on the host and insert. Photograph their position before and after the agreed carry sequence. Inspect the attachment tape, the paddle edge and the adjacent lining. Repeat after removing and reinstalling the insert. Record movement and wear instead of assuming that a tight empty fit will remain secure.

What must the removable insert drawing specify?

Show the usable paddle envelope, insertion path, anchor positions and supporting surfaces. Include the installation orientation and the agreed retention check.
