What Our Compliance Documents Actually Mean for Bag Buyers

An audit, a laboratory test and a legal registration do not prove the same thing. This guide states what Cappuccino's records mean—and what they do not mean.

Published 27 July 2026Compliance8-minute read

Direct answer: BSCI evidence relates to an audited manufacturing partner, the RoHS report relates only to submitted samples and models Cap001 and Cap001-1, and LUCID records statutory German packaging registration for Guangzhou Cappuccino Leather Handbag Co., Ltd.

Compliance documents help buyers assess risk, but only when their scope is explained correctly. Turning different records into a row of “certified” badges may look impressive, but it gives a purchasing team less useful information.

An amfori BSCI-audited manufacturing partner

Cappuccino's production network includes a manufacturing partner that has been audited under the amfori BSCI framework.

amfori describes BSCI as a system for identifying, assessing, verifying and mitigating social risks at production sites. Its official guidance also states that BSCI is not a certification. The audit applies to the named partner site within our production network. It does not mean that Guangzhou Cappuccino Leather Handbag Co., Ltd. itself should be described as a “BSCI-certified factory.”

The distinction identifies the site to which the audit evidence belongs and lets the buyer review it against the correct legal entity and location.

Restricted-substance testing for selected samples

Selected CAPPUCCINO bag models and submitted materials underwent third-party testing for the restricted substances listed in the corresponding RoHS report. The submitted samples passed the stated test limits.

This evidence is sample- and model-specific: it applies only to models Cap001 and Cap001-1 and should not be expanded into a claim that every Cappuccino product is “RoHS certified.” The European Commission describes RoHS as rules restricting hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment. For a non-electrical bag, these laboratory results are best presented as restricted-substance testing of submitted materials, not as CE certification or a universal factory certificate.

For new projects, buyers should tell us which market, construction and chemical requirements apply. Any additional test plan should be agreed for the actual materials and finished product involved.

Registration in Germany's LUCID Packaging Register

Guangzhou Cappuccino Leather Handbag Co., Ltd. is registered as a producer in Germany's LUCID Packaging Register.

The German Central Agency Packaging Register explains that LUCID is a public register for companies with obligations under German packaging law. This is evidence of statutory packaging registration. It is not a product-quality certificate and does not replace project-specific packaging, labelling or environmental obligations.

For buyers selling in Germany, the record can be relevant to supplier and packaging due diligence. Exact responsibilities still depend on who first places the packaged goods on the German market and how the supply arrangement is structured.

Why we do not publish every document openly

Original audit and registration files can contain partner-factory identities, addresses, IDs, tax information and other data that should not appear on a public marketing page.

Our website therefore presents a short scope statement. Qualified buyers can request relevant, unmodified supporting documents during supplier verification. This protects confidential information without replacing evidence with an edited certificate.

What buyers should check in any compliance claim

  • Is the document issued to the company, a partner factory or another party?
  • Does it cover a site, management system, product or submitted sample?
  • Which model, material, date and standard are listed?
  • Is the record current, and can its issuer or registry be checked?
  • Does the marketing claim match the actual scope?

These questions are more valuable than the number of logos shown on a supplier page. Our public Factory Compliance & Quality Credentials page gives the concise scope, while the buyer verification checklist shows how to use it during supplier review.

Frequently asked questions

Is amfori BSCI a certification?

No. amfori states that BSCI is not a certification. It is a social-risk-management and audit framework. Cappuccino's claim is limited to an audited manufacturing partner within its production network.

Does the RoHS report cover all Cappuccino products?

No. It applies only to the submitted samples and stated models Cap001 and Cap001-1. New materials or products may require their own assessment or testing.

Is LUCID a product certificate?

No. It is Germany's statutory packaging register. Registration should not be presented as proof of product quality.

Can qualified buyers receive the original files?

Relevant unmodified supporting documents may be provided during buyer qualification, with sensitive information handled appropriately.
