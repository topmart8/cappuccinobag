Private Label Pickleball Bags: MOQ and Sampling

Build a quantity grid by style, finish and colour before requesting a quote. Cappuccino Bag’s standard new custom bulk minimum is 300 units per style. Sample projects are assessed separately.

Find the revision cost before ordering

Define the sample scope and how changes are priced. Supply artwork, label size and rule for acceptable position shift. Ask how a colour change affects material ordering and whether packaging has its own minimum.

Approve a complete retail sample

Review the actual finish, branding, care information, stockkeeping label and packing order. Match what was approved to the bulk purchase specification and agree how deviations are escalated before shipment.

Translate a launch mix into order lines

For example, 180 backpacks and 120 slings make 300 units in total but leave both designs below the standard minimum. A 300-piece backpack program is one design to review. Ask separately whether the proposed colour split and packaging can be sourced at that quantity. Do this before purchasing labels for a launch assortment.

Does approving the artwork approve the production sample?

Artwork approval covers the signed artwork version. Review the physical placement, construction and packout on the labelled sample before bulk release.
