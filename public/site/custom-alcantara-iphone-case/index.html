A premium tactile phone accessory, engineered model by model.

A custom Alcantara iPhone case combines a visible material story with precise functional requirements. Camera openings, button access, edge height, shell tolerance and any magnetic component must be developed for the selected phone model rather than copied from a generic case.

Direct answer for buyers: Cappuccino Bag supports private-label Alcantara phone-case projects from material and model review through prototype sampling, fit checks, branding, packaging and bulk quality control. The approved sample defines the final specification.

Materials and construction.

The tactile covering, support layer and phone-case shell must behave as one system. The sample review checks bonding or assembly, edge wrapping, opening accuracy, material direction and visible finishing around the camera and buttons.

Technical review points

  • Target iPhone model and dimensional tolerance
  • Camera, button, speaker and charging access
  • Material thickness and edge construction
  • Magnetic ring position when applicable
  • Logo scale and alignment
  • Packaging fit and scratch protection

Custom Alcantara iPhone case FAQ.

Questions to resolve before a quotation and prototype.

Can you develop cases for specific iPhone models?

Yes. Identify each required model and quantity. Fit, camera opening, buttons, ports, edge height and shell construction are reviewed for that model.

Can MagSafe-related features be included?

A magnetic component can be reviewed, but attachment, alignment, charging and accessory compatibility must be tested on the approved construction before claims are made.

How is Alcantara material verified?

Sourcing is subject to project requirements and supplier documentation. Certification and origin documents can be reviewed during quotation.

Can the colour and logo match our other products?

Yes, subject to material availability and production tolerance. A coordinated colour and logo standard can connect phone cases, duffle bags and card holders.

What are the MOQ, sample time and production time?

They are confirmed according to model, construction, material availability, logo method, packaging and order quantity.

What should we send for a quotation?

Send phone model list, reference images or drawings, quantity by model and colour, material direction, logo artwork, packaging and target timing.
