What can a custom insulated cooler tote include?

A buyer can specify 20L or 30L capacity, outer fabric, PEVA-style lining, insulation, dry pockets, handles, shoulder strap, logo and retail or gift packaging. Cooling and leakage claims require an agreed sample test.

Cooler tote FAQ.

Claims and commercial terms remain project-specific.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ depends on outer fabric, lining, insulation, size, colour, logo and packaging. Share your target quantity for review.

How many hours will it keep contents cool?

We do not make an untested duration claim. Performance varies with construction, contents, ambient conditions and use, so a claim requires an agreed test.

Is it completely leakproof?

Complete leakproof performance is not promised without validated construction and testing. We can review lining and seam directions for the intended contents.
