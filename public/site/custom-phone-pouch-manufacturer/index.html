Custom Phone Pouch Manufacturer

Cappuccino Bag is a custom phone pouch manufacturer for phone pouches, crossbody phone bags and compact lifestyle accessories for European fashion, travel and tech accessory brands.

Direct Answer for Buyers

Cappuccino Bag manufactures custom phone pouches and crossbody phone bags with OEM/ODM support for size, strap type, card slots, zipper pockets, vegan leather or coated canvas materials, logo customization and retail-ready packaging.

Buyer Pain Points

  • Phone pouch dimensions must work across target phone models and cases.
  • Straps, hardware and stitching need load-bearing checks for daily use.
  • Brands need fashionable styling without losing practical storage.
  • Small-batch sampling must be clear before moving to bulk seasonal orders.

Product Options

  • Vertical phone pouches
  • Crossbody phone bags
  • Phone pouches with card slots
  • Phone pouches with zipper coin pockets
  • Adjustable strap phone bags
  • Matching RFID cardholders, key pouches and passport holders

Materials and Logo Customization

Material choices can include vegan leather, PU, nylon, coated canvas, recycled fabric directions, microfiber lining and leather detail trims. Logo methods include debossing, foil stamping, screen printing, heat transfer, woven label, metal logo and custom hangtags.

Suitable Buyers

Suitable buyers include lifestyle brands, fashion accessory brands, travel retailers, museum stores, hotel groups, promotional gift companies, European e-commerce brands and tech accessory distributors.

OEM/ODM Process

  1. Share target phone size, style reference, strap idea, material and logo requirements.
  2. Confirm dimensions, pocket layout, hardware color, strap width and packaging.
  3. Develop sample and check phone fit, strap comfort, stitching and logo accuracy.
  4. Approve production sample, packing method and QC points.
  5. Produce bulk order with inspection photos, packing confirmation and shipment support.

FAQ

Can phone pouches include card slots?

Yes. Card slots, RFID pockets, zipper pockets and passport-size variations can be reviewed during sampling.

Can straps be customized?

Yes. Strap length, width, material, adjuster, hardware color and detachable function can be customized.

Can you make a matched accessory collection?

Yes. Phone pouches can be developed with cardholders, key pouches, passport holders and gift packaging.
