How an Alcantara OEM project moves from design to production.

A clear approval path reduces colour, logo-position and structure errors before bulk production.

01

Project brief

Share reference images or a technical pack, target customer, quantity, colour direction, logo files, packaging and destination market.

02

Material review

Review available material references, supplier documentation, colour, backing, thickness and compatibility with the proposed structure.

03

Pattern development

Translate the concept into patterns, reinforcement zones, seam construction and estimated material consumption.

04

Prototype sample

Build and review the first sample for appearance, fit, hand feel, function, logo position and workmanship.

05

Approved specification

Consolidate revisions into a pre-production sample and approved specification sheet before line preparation.

06

Production and shipment

Complete cutting, sewing or assembly, in-line and final quality checks, packing, carton marking and export release.

Buyer questions about Alcantara development.

These answers help define a quotation without assuming fixed material availability, MOQ or lead time.

Can Alcantara colours and logos be customized?

Colour availability follows the qualified supply route. Product colour coordination, lining, edge paint, hardware, packaging and suitable logo methods can be developed around the selected material and approved sample.

How are material origin and specifications verified?

Material sourcing is reviewed during quotation against project requirements and available supplier documentation. Origin or certification claims are made only when the relevant documents can be reviewed for that order.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send product type, reference images or technical pack, target dimensions, estimated quantity, colour and material direction, logo artwork, packaging requirement, target market and timing.

Can the three products share one colour and packaging system?

Yes, subject to material availability and construction review. A coordinated colour, logo and packaging system can connect duffle bags, phone cases and card holders while their technical structures remain product-specific.

What are the MOQ, sample time and production time?

They are confirmed according to design, material availability, logo method, packaging and order quantity. The quotation records the project-specific starting quantity and schedule.
