Common Buyer Questions

Common Buyer Questions

Can you make custom travel weekender bags & duffel bags from my reference photo?

Yes. Cappuccino Bags can review your reference photo, tech pack or sample, then suggest structure, materials, logo method, sample options and production details.

What customization options are available?

We can customize material, size, compartments, lining, hardware, zipper, strap, logo method, label, packaging and carton marks based on your target market.

What is the MOQ for this product?

MOQ depends on material, structure, logo method and packaging. Send your product requirement and estimated quantity so we can suggest a realistic starting option.

How long does sample development take?

Sampling time depends on product complexity, material availability and logo method. Functional sports bags and smart products usually need more review than simple bags.

Can you provide factory photos and QC photos?

Yes. Buyers can request workshop photos, sample videos, QC inspection photos, packing photos and carton mark confirmation before shipment.
