Choose the right padel bag format

A strong padel bag starts with the way players actually carry equipment: rackets, shoes, towels, apparel, water bottles and smaller personal items.

Confirm structure before sampling

Ask the factory to confirm racket capacity, thermal lining options, shoe compartment ventilation, waterproof base fabric, zipper grade, logo method and carton packing.

Plan private label details

For private-label programs, prepare a brief with target retail price, material preference, expected MOQ, reference photos, market region and packaging requirements.

FAQ

What should a padel bag brief include?

Include target quantity, racket capacity, shoe pocket requirement, material direction, logo method, packaging and target market.

Can padel bags have thermal lining?

Yes. Thermal lining options can be reviewed during sample development.
