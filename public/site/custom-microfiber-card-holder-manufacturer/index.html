Microfiber suede card holders for private-label programs

Cappuccino Bag supports OEM/ODM microfiber suede card holders, RFID card cases, compact wallets and passport holder sets for accessory brands, travel retailers, automotive-inspired lifestyle buyers and corporate gift programs.

Projects can start from a reference photo, sketch, tech pack or retail target. The development route covers material handfeel, card slot count, lining, edge finishing, logo method, RFID layer, packaging and carton planning.

Alcantara-style wording for accurate public SEO

Use Alcantara-style microfiber suede or premium microfiber suede as the default public wording. These phrases communicate the premium soft-touch direction without implying that every product uses genuine Alcantara®.

Genuine Alcantara® should only be discussed when a buyer specifies verified sourcing and provides material proof for that specific project.

Sample review details

Card holder samples should be reviewed for slot tension, edge consistency, lining alignment, RFID layer placement, logo clarity, microfiber nap direction, corner durability and packaging presentation.
