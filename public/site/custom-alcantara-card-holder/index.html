Small accessories where material and workmanship remain highly visible.

Card holders use little material, but buyers see every edge, stitch line, slot opening and logo position. Development therefore focuses on clean pattern accuracy, controlled thickness, card fit and finishing as much as the tactile surface.

Direct answer for buyers: Cappuccino Bag develops custom Alcantara card holders, folding wallets and passport-cover formats through material inspection, pattern making, sample construction, fit review, buyer approval, bulk production and final quality control.

Materials and construction.

Slot edges, fold lines and corners experience repeated handling. The material, backing and internal support need to create the required hand feel without making the finished card holder unnecessarily bulky.

Sample review points

  • Card insertion and removal
  • Slot depth, spacing and retention
  • Fold behaviour and finished thickness
  • Edge, corner and stitch consistency
  • Logo position and legibility
  • Colour consistency and packaging fit

OEM/ODM development process.

A controlled small-accessory process reduces slot, edge and logo-position variations in bulk production.

01

Buyer brief

Confirm format, dimensions, slot count, target cards or passport, quantity, colour, logo and packaging.

02

Material review

Assess sourcing documentation, colour, backing, thickness, reinforcement and any RFID requirement.

03

Pattern making

Develop slot angles, overlap, fold allowances, edge construction and material consumption.

04

Prototype sample

Construct the first sample and review fit, thickness, slot use, edges, stitches, logo and packaging.

05

Approval

Record revisions in the pre-production sample and approved specification sheet.

06

Production and QC

Control cutting, skiving or thickness preparation, stitching, finishing, inspection and packing.

Quality control, packaging and global shipping.

Inspection can cover incoming material, colour, thickness, cut accuracy, card-slot dimensions, stitching, edge finishing, fold alignment, logo placement, cleanliness and packaging. An RFID layer, when requested, is evaluated within the approved construction rather than assumed as a standard claim.

Custom Alcantara card holder FAQ.

Questions that help the factory prepare a practical quotation.

Can you develop both card holders and passport covers?

Yes. Share the required formats, dimensions, slot count and intended contents so the pattern and sample can be developed accordingly.

Can RFID shielding be included?

An RFID layer can be evaluated when required. Its effect on thickness, fold and slot construction is reviewed during sampling.

How is Alcantara sourcing verified?

Supplier documentation can be reviewed during quotation. No authorization, certification or Italian-origin claim is made without project-specific evidence.

Which logo methods can be used?

Embossing, debossing, printing, laser marking or a small metal logo may be reviewed according to material, backing, thickness and the sample result.

What are the MOQ, sample time and production time?

They are confirmed according to construction, material availability, logo, RFID requirement, packaging and order quantity.

Can the packaging match a duffle bag or phone-case collection?

Yes. The Alcantara Collection can use coordinated colours, logos, inserts and gift-box styling across product categories.
