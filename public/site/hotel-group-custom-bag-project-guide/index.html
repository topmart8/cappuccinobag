FAQ

Common Hotel Project Questions

Can you make custom hotel backpacks and travel bags from our reference photo or design brief?

Yes. Cappuccino Bags can review your design brief, reference photo, sample or tech pack, then suggest material options, logo methods, structure details and production planning for hotel projects.

What products can be included in a hotel bag project?

We can support travel backpacks, weekender bags, trolley luggage, staff bags, guest gift bags, RFID wallets, passport holders and branded accessory sets depending on your project scope.

Can you match hotel brand colors and packaging requirements?

Yes. We can review Pantone references, logo usage, packaging details, carton marks and guest-facing presentation needs during the development process.

Can you support large-volume or multi-property hotel programs?

Yes. We can support project-based development, production scheduling, repeat-order coordination and export communication for large-volume hospitality programs.

Can you provide production proof before bulk shipment?

Yes. Buyers can request sample photos, workshop photos, QC inspection photos, packing confirmation and shipping communication support before final shipment.
