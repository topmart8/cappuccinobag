Custom Pickleball Accessories

Develop soft-goods accessories such as paddle covers, ball pouches and shoe sacks for your court collection.

Select soft goods by fit

Send a trace or dimensioned paddle outline for a cover. For a ball pouch, specify the ball count and opening style. For a shoe sack, provide the largest paired footprint and sole height, with a packed-fit photo if available.

Define the bundle and labelling

Specify whether each accessory is sold alone, packed inside a bag or added to a retail kit. Provide artwork placement and target quantities separately for each item. Accessory minimums and packaging feasibility are assessed by configuration.

Buyer question

Can I source a pouch alongside a bag program?

Yes, submit the pouch and bag as distinct line items. Review both the pouch fit and bundle packing before approving the order.

Standard custom production starts at 300 pieces per design. Each design has its own minimum. Sampling is reviewed separately.
