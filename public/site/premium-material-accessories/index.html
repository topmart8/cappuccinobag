Premium accessories for private-label buyers

Cappuccino Bag supports custom premium material accessories for private-label buyers, travel brands, automotive-inspired lifestyle brands and gift programs. Our range includes microfiber suede card holders, Alcantara-style wallets, RFID card cases, passport holders, phone cases, small pouches and travel organizers. Buyers can customize material, color, logo, lining, packaging and retail presentation.

Material wording and sourcing clarity

Default product wording should use premium microfiber suede or Alcantara-style microfiber suede. This keeps the public page accurate for suede-look accessory programs while leaving room for verified buyer-specified materials.

Only use genuine Alcantara® when the buyer provides verified material sourcing, documentation and proof for that project. Cappuccino Bag does not claim that all premium material accessories are genuine Alcantara®.

Accessory range and customization

The product range can include card holders, RFID card cases, slim wallets, passport holders, phone cases, small pouches, travel organizers and coordinated gift sets. Buyers can choose card slot layout, RFID layer, snap or zipper closure, microfiber handfeel, lining, edge finishing, logo method and packaging.
