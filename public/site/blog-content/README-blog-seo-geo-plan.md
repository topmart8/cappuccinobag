## GEO Optimization Notes

To improve citation potential in search engines and buyer research tools:

- Keep the direct answer within 40 to 60 words.
- Use factual definitions before persuasive copy.
- Put comparison tables near the top half of the article.
- Use "In our factory experience..." notes to strengthen EEAT.
- Keep FAQ answers concise and direct.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as unverified certifications or invented customer names.
