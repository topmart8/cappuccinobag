# Competitor Reference Notes

## What to Borrow Strategically

- Use detailed SEO category entry points by design, material, craft, and buyer type.
- Speak directly to new brands and designers who need help turning ideas into production-ready bags.
- Make quotation actions obvious across the site: Get a Quote, Request Samples, Download Catalog, WhatsApp.
- Show factory and quality proof without unsupported certification claims.
- Build separate landing pages for paid search intent instead of sending every ad to the homepage.

## What Not to Copy

- Do not copy competitor layouts, images, text, slogans, or product descriptions.
- Do not overuse generic luxury wording if it is not supported by product photography.
- Do not display CE/BSCI certificate visuals until files are provided.
- Do not make all content about the company; every page should answer a buyer question.

## Cappuccino Differentiation

- Position Cappuccino around 16 years of premium bag manufacturing plus OEM/ODM flexibility.
- Lead with Fashion Women’s Bags, then support the offer with outdoor performance and eco-tech innovation.
- Use the uploaded video as trust proof on Home and Factory sections.
- Build SEO/AIO/GEO content around procurement questions, material comparisons, private-label production, RFID wallets, and GPS trackable bags.
