FAQ

Buyer Questions

What types of outdoor multifunctional bags can you make?

We can develop outdoor sports bags, hiking backpacks, mountaineering backpacks, trekking backpacks, gym bags, duffel bags, travel bags, racket bags and multifunctional backpacks with custom materials, compartments, logos and packaging.

Can you make custom tennis bags with racket compartments?

Yes. We can develop tennis bags with racket compartments, shoe pockets, side pockets, shoulder straps, durable fabrics and private-label branding based on your reference photo or tech pack.

Can you make pickleball and padel bags with shoe pockets?

Yes. Pickleball and padel bags can be customized with paddle or racket compartments, shoe pockets, wet/dry separation, multi-pocket layouts, logo details and retail-ready packaging.

Can you customize outdoor sports bags with water-resistant fabric?

Yes. We can suggest nylon, polyester, coated fabric and other durable materials depending on your target price, market and functional requirements.

Can you make hiking backpacks or mountaineering backpacks?

Yes. We can support hiking, trekking and mountaineering backpack projects with reinforced stitching, ergonomic straps, bottle pockets, laptop compartments and practical storage structures.

Can you make travel bags and trolley luggage for hotel groups?

Yes. We support hotel groups and hospitality buyers with travel bags, trolley luggage, guest gifts, staff bags, RFID wallets, passport holders and branded travel products.

Can you make wallets, RFID wallets or card holders?

Yes. We can make wallets, RFID security wallets, card holders, passport holders, coin purses, phone pouches, key pouches and gift sets with custom logos and packaging.

Can you make GPS trackable bags?

Yes. We can support GPS-ready bag development by planning tracker pockets, smart security details, material choices and product structure for travel, outdoor or lifestyle use.

What sustainable materials can you offer?

Depending on project requirements, we can discuss vegan leather, recycled fabric, apple leather, Alcantara, microfiber and other eco-tech material options.

What is your MOQ?

MOQ depends on product type, material, logo method and customization level. We can review your project and suggest suitable sample and bulk order options.

How long does sampling take?

Sampling time depends on product complexity, material availability and logo method. Simple projects are faster, while multifunctional bags or smart products need more review.

Can you add my private logo?

Yes. Logo methods include embossing, debossing, printing, metal logo, woven label, rubber patch, zipper puller branding and hot stamping.

Can you support Amazon and Shopify sellers?

Yes. We can support packaging requirements, barcode labels, carton marks, repeat orders and product details for Amazon, Shopify and DTC sellers.

Can you support large-volume customers?

Yes. We can support bulk production, private-label packaging, confidential projects, production updates and long-term supply programs.

Can you provide factory photos, sample videos and QC inspection photos?

Yes. Buyers can request workshop photos, sample videos, QC inspection photos, packing photos and carton mark confirmation before shipment.

Can you arrange shipping?

We can support export communication, carton details and shipment preparation. Final shipping method can be discussed according to destination and buyer requirements.
