Brand Product Development

Start with an idea, references or a tech pack. Build a clear specification before committing to physical sampling. Custom MOQ is 300 pcs per style; special combinations require manual review.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Define brand positioning, intended users, reference images, target cost, expected quantity and launch date. No design files required.

  2. 02

    Material Selection

    Review swatches, structure, color, lining and trims against the brief. Confirm availability and documentation for the selected material.

  3. 03

    Design Approval

    Confirm the written design and specification. A concept image or AI visualization is not formal design approval. Sample fee authorization is a separate decision.

  4. 04

    Sampling

    After design approval and separate agreement on sample scope and any fee, develop the physical sample. No automatic sample PI or charge.

  5. 05

    Revision

    Record fit, construction, color and branding changes. Approve the revised physical sample and specification before bulk production.

  6. 06

    Bulk Production & QC

    Confirm the order and agreed inspection plan. Check materials, construction, function, labels and packing against approved references.

Development questions

Can I start without design files?

Yes. Share brand positioning, authorized reference images, material preferences, target cost, expected quantity and launch date. Unknown details can be discussed during discovery.

When does paid sampling start?

Design approval comes first. Physical sampling and any sample fee require a separate agreement. Submitting an RFQ does not authorize a sample PI, charge or production.

What is the custom MOQ?

Custom MOQ is 300 pcs per style. Special quantities and mixed combinations require manual review and are not automatically accepted.

Do you organize events?

No. Clubs and events solutions cover manufacturing and supplier coordination for bags, selected accessories and packaging, not event operation.

Can existing customers reorder?

Yes. Select existing-customer reorder and provide the prior order or approved sample reference. Specifications, material availability, quantity and timing are rechecked before confirmation.
