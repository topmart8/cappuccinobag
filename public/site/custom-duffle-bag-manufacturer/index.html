OEM/ODM duffle bags for travel and lifestyle buyers

Cappuccino Bag develops custom duffle bags for travel brands, gym chains, hotel groups, school teams, sports clubs and private-label lifestyle programs. The same basic product shape can become a promotional gym bag, a refined weekender bag, a hotel welcome gift, or a premium retail travel duffle depending on the structure, material and branding choices.

Buyers can request polyester, nylon, coated fabric, vegan leather, premium microfiber suede, or mixed-material construction. For premium programs, microfiber suede panels and soft-touch trims can create a refined travel/accessory feeling without making unsupported material claims.

Custom structure, logo and packaging options

Common options include U-shaped openings, shoe tunnels, wet pockets, trolley sleeves, detachable straps, reinforced bottoms, custom zipper pullers, branded labels, woven patches, embossing, lining colors, hang tags, barcode labels, gift boxes and export cartons.

For hotel gift and travel retail programs, early packaging planning is important because folded size, carton quantity, shape recovery and retail presentation all affect the final cost and customer experience.
