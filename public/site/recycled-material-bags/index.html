Cappuccino develops custom bags using project-selected recycled polyester and recycled nylon options. We review the material together with the lining, reinforcement, branding and intended use before sampling.

Review Your Bag Brief

Which recycled fabric fits your bag?

Start with how the bag will be carried, loaded and stored. A folding shopping tote, a travel organizer and a structured racket bag require different fabrics and construction. Recycled content is one selection factor alongside weight, surface, seams and reinforcement.

A fine recycled polyester twill can be reviewed for a smooth, comfortable textile character and a coordinated colour range. Lightweight nylon options can be considered when folding volume and low bulk are priorities. Physical swatches establish the appearance and handle; abrasion, tear and water-related performance need the selected specification and test evidence.

What can be customized?

Review dimensions, compartments, lining, strap layout, base support, zipper, logo method and packaging together. For sports bags, define racket or equipment space and any shoe separation. For travel accessories, define packing capacity, pocket access and the packed size.

How do recycled content and GRS documentation work?

Recycled polyester and recycled nylon have different material sources. The selected shell, lining and other components should each be identified. Supplier documentation may support a material review, but it does not automatically certify Cappuccino or the completed bag. The selected article, current scope and relevant order documentation are checked before any certification claim is agreed.

What happens before bulk production?

Agree the textile, colour and finish; develop the prototype; review seams, load points, branding and packing; then record the approved sample and specifications. Material water repellency alone does not make the completed bag waterproof.

New custom bulk orders start at 300 pieces per style. Different styles cannot be combined to reach this minimum. Colour splits and special materials may require higher quantities; samples are assessed separately.

Frequently asked questions

Is recycled nylon the same as RPET?

No. Nylon is polyamide; RPET is recycled polyester. Each needs its own composition and recycled-source documentation. A PET bottle illustration does not establish the origin of nylon.

Does water-repellent fabric make a waterproof bag?

No. Water repellency describes surface behaviour. Seams, zippers, coating and construction determine the finished bag performance and must be reviewed and tested for the intended claim.

Can I use these fabrics for a foldable bag?

Lightweight recycled nylon or selected recycled polyester can be evaluated for packable bags. Confirm fabric weight, folded dimensions, seams, reinforcement and the intended carrying load on the prototype.

What is the minimum order?

New custom bulk orders start at 300 pieces per style. Different styles cannot be combined to reach this minimum. Colour splits and special materials may require higher quantities; samples are assessed separately.
