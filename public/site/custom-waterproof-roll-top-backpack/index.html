What can a custom waterproof roll-top backpack include?

A buyer can specify 25L or 35L capacity, coated material direction, closure geometry, attachment points, padding, hardware, colour, logo and packaging. Final weather-protection claims are tied to the approved construction and test—not assumed from fabric alone.

Adjustable roll-top closure

Opening circumference, fold count, buckle position and access are refined around the target capacity and weather-protection goal.

TPU, PVC or recycled coated options

Coated fabric directions can be reviewed against hand feel, colour, price, weld or seam construction and testing requirements.

Reinforced outdoor carry

Base panel, shoulder straps, back padding, top handle and load points are developed for the intended contents.

Roll-top backpack FAQ.

Project-specific answers are confirmed in the quotation and sample approval.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ depends on coated material, colour, capacity, buckle, logo and packaging. Send the estimated quantity for a realistic starting option.

Is the backpack submersible?

No submersion or complete-waterproof claim is made without validated construction and an agreed test. Materials, seams and closure are developed to the target requirement.

Can both sizes share one design language?

Yes. Colours, materials, attachment points and branding can coordinate while strap, base and pattern details are adjusted for each capacity.
