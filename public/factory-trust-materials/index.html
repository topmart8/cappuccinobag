Evidence with defined boundaries

Three records, three different purposes

An audit, a laboratory report and a statutory registration do not prove the same thing. The notes below keep each claim tied to its documented scope.

01

Responsible Manufacturing Network

Our production network includes a manufacturing partner audited under the amfori BSCI framework.

Scope: The audit applies to a manufacturing partner and does not represent direct certification of Guangzhou Cappuccino Leather Handbag Co., Ltd.

02

Product Compliance Testing

Selected CAPPUCCINO bag models and submitted materials have undergone third-party restricted-substance testing and passed the limits stated in the corresponding RoHS report.

Scope: Testing applies only to the submitted samples and stated models Cap001 and Cap001-1.

03

German Packaging Registration

Guangzhou Cappuccino Leather Handbag Co., Ltd. is registered as a producer in Germany's LUCID Packaging Register.

Scope: LUCID is a statutory packaging registration, not a product certification.
